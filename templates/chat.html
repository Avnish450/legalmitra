How the answer is found

Your question is cleaned, stemmed and turned into a TF-IDF vector, then compared by cosine similarity against every topic and template in the knowledge base. The closest match is explained back to you.

🔊

How the voice works

Speech recognition and speech synthesis both run inside your browser using the Web Speech API. Nothing is sent to a paid speech service, and it works best in Chrome or Edge.

🔐

What is stored

Only the question and the answer, so we can show usage analytics and give the bot a short memory of the conversation. No name, no login, no personal data.

Important: LegalMitra gives general legal information for learning. It cannot replace an advocate, it does not know the facts of your case, and it must not be relied on for any decision with legal consequences.
